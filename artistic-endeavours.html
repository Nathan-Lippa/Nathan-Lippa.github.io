Radio is Dead

In 2024, I was a part of the revitalization effort to bring Radio is Dead back in-house at Radio CKUT in Montréal. Every week, we perform one hour of improvised music of all kinds. We've made a few compilation projects over the years. The one below was made from episodes at the end of 2025.
