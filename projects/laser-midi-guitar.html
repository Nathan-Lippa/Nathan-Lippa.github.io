Musical Instrument Design

Date: October 2025 | Tags: Hardware, C++, CAD

Overview

This project involved designing a custom digital musical instrument from scratch. The goal was to create an ergonomic, highly responsive controller that could map physical inputs to MIDI outputs efficiently.
